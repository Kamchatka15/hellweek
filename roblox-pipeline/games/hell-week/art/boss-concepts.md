# Boss concept script — for Grok Imagine

Copy a block, paste it, generate. Each block is self-contained.

## Read this first

**The deer problem.** 99 Nights' owned monster is a deer standing on two legs. Their own intro comic
opens with it. It is the first item on our do-not-copy list. So the lead boss below keeps the
*grammar* of a too-tall antlered biped and changes the *animal*: a desert antelope with long straight
horns rather than a forest deer with branching antlers. Straight horns read as blades, they suit the
obelisk, and nobody will mistake it for theirs. A deer-leaning variant is kept at the end for
comparison only.

**The celebrity.** Must not resemble any real person. No real name, no recognisable face, no actual
brand. Generic fame signifiers only.

## The boss grammar

Every boss here obeys the same six rules. That is what makes them a family rather than six unrelated
monsters, and it is what "mirroring" means in practice.

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **3x player height.** About 18 studs against a 6-stud avatar | Big enough to read over scenery, small enough to fit a doorway shot |
| 2 | **Exactly one wrong proportion** | One wrong thing is uncanny. Three wrong things is just a cartoon |
| 3 | **A glowing signature you read before the body** | At 40 studs in a navy night you see the glow first. Same principle as the scorpion's eyes |
| 4 | **A silhouette you can name in one word** from the fog | If you cannot name it in one word it will not make a clip |
| 5 | **It never runs** | Walking slowly is worse. It is also the genre rule: the threat is a pane of glass, not a chase |
| 6 | **Something trailing** — cloth, dust, smell, light | So it reads as moving even when it is standing still |

# 1 — THE LEAD. The Dry Herald

The boss everything else mirrors.

**Design in one line:** a sun-bleached desert antelope, standing wrong, wrapped in linen, with a
crown of straight horns and no face left.

- **Wrong proportion:** the neck. Far too long, and it holds the head too high, like something
  looking over a wall it should not be able to see over
- **Glowing signature:** two points of hot gold where the eyes should be, behind the wrapping
- **One word:** *horns*
- **Trailing:** loose linen and a thin fall of sand pouring from the joints

# How to run these

1. **Silhouette first.** Run prompt B for the lead before anything else. If the shape is not
   nameable in one word, the rest is decoration on a bad idea.
2. **Four images per prompt, then pick one.** Do not iterate on a prompt until you have seen its
   spread.
3. **Judge at thumbnail size.** Shrink the result until it is an inch tall. That is how a player sees
   it through fog. If it turns to mush, it fails rule 4.
4. **Check the glow reads.** Squint until the image is almost dark. The signature should be the last
   thing still visible.
5. **Keep what fails.** A rejected boss is usually a good normal creature.

## What comes back into the build

We do not upload generated images as game assets. Concept art decides **silhouette, proportion,
palette and the glow signature**, and those four numbers get built as parts by a generator, the same
way the obelisk and the scorpion were. The art is the brief, not the asset.
