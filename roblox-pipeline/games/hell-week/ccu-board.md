## Reading the board
- **The genre has one anchor and a long tail.** 99 Nights is 3× the next comp and 4× DOORS. Everything below #3 is under 25K. Hell Week's realistic first target is the Dead Rails floor (~20K) shape, not the anchor.
- **Like ratio tracks pacing, not fear.** DOORS (93%) and Dead Rails (92.6%) are the best-liked; Forsaken (84.5%) is PvP-asymmetric and Pressure (86.7%) is the heaviest tone. Mild fear with fair pacing is the liked corner — the brief's 9+ register is the right one.
- **Every live comp ships on a visible schedule.** 99 Nights biweekly Saturdays, Dead Rails teasing 09-19, DOORS teasing 09-18, Forsaken weekly "WEEK n" tiles. Pressure, the one without a cadence, is the one at 1% of peak.
- **Rolimon's "24-h" reads run higher than the storefront's live figure** (DOORS 71K vs 59K, Forsaken 125K vs 82K). Use the storefront for "now", Rolimon's for peaks and totals.
