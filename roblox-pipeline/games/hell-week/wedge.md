# Wedge

99 Nights: gather and hide.
Hell Week: gather, hide, and the easy pile is a trap.

The Gift (Day 3+) sits on the fog line with a wrong warm bloom.
Take it: fuel now, worse tomorrow.
That is the clip and the only extra hook in Pass 1.

Implemented in Pass 1 as `config.lure` (`server/config.luau`): appears on the light-line from Day 3, worth 5 fuel, Weight +1, next night ×1.25 burn per Weight, stacking. Weight is drawn only as small marks under the Day badge from Day 3 — never a fourth HUD number.
