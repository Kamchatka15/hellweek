# Hell Week — theme (five style lines, adopted from the packet)


1. Silhouette: low-poly, single-colour, readable at 200px
2. Ground + accent: grey sand / black water + fire gold
3. Light source: the Wick. Gift bloom is the only competing warm
4. Eye track: Wick, one fuel pile, Day number
5. Mood: hush, mild fear, zero gore, zero sermon

Player-facing zone name: **The Quiet Shore**
Do not label the map Sheol / Hades / Gehenna.
Register (designers only): quiet grave / underworld, not a torture chamber.

Colours are pinned in `shared/theme.luau` (`colors.*`). The Gift bloom (`colors.lure`) is a pink-orange that is deliberately not the wick gold — the wrongness is the tell.

Opening act (owner amendment 2026-09-12): narration in GothamBlack pale grey, the voice in Creepster blood-red `168,8,8` with dark stroke and running drips, the meaning in Garamond `196,186,170`. Narration is ~1.6× the voice on purpose.

The light is the obelisk's cap fire (owner amendment 2026-09-12). No candles, no wax, no post anywhere on the shore; every flame is the HW-002 jet (obelisk cap, bench edge).
