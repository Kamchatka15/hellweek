# PLAYBOOK — laws of winning Roblox games

Compounding asset of the research skill. Notes in `research/` are evidence; this is the distillate. Rules: laws are stated so they'd hold in a different game; each carries its evidence; new notes strengthen, contradict, or merge laws — never just append. Keep under ~2 pages.

## Laws

1. **Wrap the slot machine in a skill shell.** Put a timing/aiming minigame between action and random reward — randomness feels earned, grinding feels like practicing. *(mining-genre-winners, 2026-09-11: DIG sweet spots, Prospecting panning)*
2. **Make the grind stat the jackpot stat.** When the stat you upgrade is the one that improves rare rolls, progression and anticipation compound. *(mining-genre-winners: Prospecting Luck, DIG shovel Luck)*
3. **Loss risk prices excitement.** A bust condition that costs the current haul turns repetition into push-your-luck decisions. Stakes, not punishment — pair with a comeback loop. *(mining-genre-winners: DIG endurance bar; Layer Mine's death ore-drop already implements this, plus recovery)*
4. **Always show the next scarce thing.** Visible, time-and-place-limited opportunities (beacon spots, meteor events, boss drops) move players through the world and create shared moments. *(mining-genre-winners)*
5. **Collections are the second game.** A completion axis orthogonal to currency means no session ends empty. *(mining-genre-winners: Prospecting museum, mutation hunting)*
6. **New layer, new rules.** Progression tiers must change the game (new tables, new hazards), not just scale numbers, or depth becomes a treadmill. *(mining-genre-winners)*
7. **The loop starts in under 60 seconds.** Core action before tutorial, story, or shop — and since the June 2026 discovery rework pays for retention and session quality, the first minute is acquisition strategy. *(mining-genre-winners: SM Games + PIPELINE.md)*
8. **They pay to smooth a loop they already love.** Friction removal, pace, identity — never power over others, always legible enough for a kid to explain the purchase. *(mining-genre-winners: Sell Anywhere-type passes; house rule: understood purpose)*
9. **Retention machine, never coercion machine.** Events, streaks, collections yes; panic timers, doomscroll loops, gambling-shaped paid randomness no — both because of the Aug 2026 enforcement climate and because generosity-coded games hold the revenue records. *(PIPELINE.md ground rules; mining-genre-winners policy flags)*
