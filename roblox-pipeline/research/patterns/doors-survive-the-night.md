## Notes

### The two things Hell Week actually takes from DOORS
1. **Cadence, not content.** DOORS proves a 9+ audience will sit in sustained tension if every threat has a *readable* tell and a *learnable* answer, and if the big scares land on a schedule the player can feel (10 / 30 / 50 / 80 / 100). Hell Week's schedule is Day 1 watch → Night 2 chase → Day 3 Gift → Day 5 → Day 7. Same skeleton, seven beats instead of a hundred doors.
2. **The first purchase is a rescue, not a key.** 30 R$ at the moment of loss, one per run, blocked when it would be unfair. Second Wind is exactly this SKU. The 120 R$ five-pack is the *second* purchase and exists for people who already bought the first one.

### Numbers worth keeping
- Welcome 194,283 / Welcome Back 91,323 won on the same day. Different cohorts, but as a rough D1 return signal it is ~47% — for a four-year-old horror game. A named next-day badge costs nothing; put one in Pass 1 ("Second Dawn").
- Buddy System 89,563/day ≈ 46% of first-joins play with a friend. Co-op is the default mode, not the bonus.
- 93.0% likes with 352K dislikes; the public complaints cluster on Floor 2 and on one player ruining the group — both are pacing problems, not content problems.
