### What the numbers say
- 286K "survive 10 days" badges in one day against ~260K CCU means the average player is *finishing* short runs, not idling — a run that ends is the retention engine, not the enemy. Hell Week's 7-day run is the same idea compressed.
- The diamond price ladder flattens from 4.95 to 3.57 R$/diamond — a 28% volume discount, gentle. Copy the slope, not the SKUs.
- Like ratio 90.5% with 564K dislikes: griefing and difficulty are the two sources public complaints name. Both are removed by design in Hell Week (personal sack, 4-player cap, run ≤ 15 min).
- The brief's §2 claim "Deer skips night 1" is verified. The brief's "~3 min day / 1.5 min night" is verified exactly (3:00 / 1:30).
