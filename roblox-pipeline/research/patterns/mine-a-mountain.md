## Notes
The most important line in this teardown for us is the wave stage. This shape works — 131K peak, 97% likes — and the wave is over. Fat Man Gets Rich is the *same shape with a better hook* (the flood is a clip; a pickaxe swing is not), entering after the wave. That only works if the social/clip loop is real, because the shape alone has already been done.
