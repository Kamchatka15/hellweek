# Teardown — Bee Swarm Simulator (Onett) — the bag-and-bank archetype — 2026-09-12

> Public sources: storefront (4.6B visits, 96% rating, ~20K CCU on the LevelUpPlay snapshot), the game's own wiki, seven years of public gameplay. Specific Robux prices are not asserted here — *verify at G2 from the storefront*. Doctrine §3.1: copy the shape.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Category | **collect** — the oldest, most refined bag-and-bank loop on the platform |
| Numbers now | ~20K CCU (snapshot) · 4.6B visits · 96% likes · **evergreen since 2018** |
| Wave stage | **Evergreen.** Not a wave. The proof that the shape, done well, holds for years |
| **First 60 seconds** | Spawn at your hive → walk to the nearest flower field → hold to collect pollen → backpack fills → **walk back to the hive to convert pollen into honey** → honey buys your first egg/bee → more bees = faster pollen. First conversion inside the first minute |
| **Core verb + reward cadence** | Collect (pollen). Pollen/second scales with bees, tools and bag. Bag fills in ~1–3 min early; **the trip back is the rhythm of the game** |
| **Meta loop** | Bees as a collection (hatch, level, rarity); fields unlock by bee count; tools and bags ladder; quests from NPC bears; badges |
| **Social loop** | Moderate: shared fields, "meet the same players," group bosses, gifting bees. Not trade-heavy |
| **Return hook** | Honey tokens, daily quests, timed boss respawns, ticket events. Bees keep "working" symbolically but **no offline earnings** — a deliberate choice that keeps play necessary |
| Event cadence | Seasonal events (Beesmas etc.), otherwise slow, deliberate updates |
| **Monetization catalog** | Robux shop: eggs (rare bees), Royal Jelly, honey packs, night bells, magic beans, tickets, limited packs. Shape = **accelerate the collection you already love + rare bees others can see** |
| **Complaints = the wedge** | Slow early (before a few bees, pollen is a crawl); update cadence; heavy grind past mid-game |
| **Style spec** | Bright, flat, saturated flower fields; soft rounded shapes; a *big* readable bag meter; hive as the visual "home" you return to; cheerful, no darkness |
| Creator Store equivalents | search: `low poly flower field`, `stylized bag icon`, `honey drop` — we do not need bees, we need the *return-to-home* silhouette |
| **Copy this** | 1 · **the trip back is the rhythm** — make the bank walk feel good (path, VFX, sound), do not shorten it to zero. 2 · a **big bag meter** everyone can read. 3 · the **collection you can see** (bees) — Fat Man's version is the coin pile / status goods. 4 · **no early wall**: the first egg is cheap and immediate |
| **Do not copy this** | The seven-year depth (a first slice is not this), no offline earnings (our return hook is offline — but note *why* BSS skipped it), any asset or name |

## Notes
Bee Swarm is why "bag-and-bank" is a genre and not a gimmick. Its lesson for the economy sim's finding: **it never lets the bank trip become pointless.** Our offline earnings at 4/hr made the trip pointless by day 2. Candidate C fixes the number; this teardown says the *feel* of the trip is the thing to protect.
