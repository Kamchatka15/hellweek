# Pattern: Live / recurring events

## What it is
Scheduled, time-boxed content that happens AT a time — a weekly update drop, a recurring giveaway hour, a season, a one-off concert.

## Why it works (the psychology)
- **Appointment mechanic** — a reason to log in at a specific time; converts "maybe later" into a calendar habit.
- **Novelty cadence** — a fresh thing to want on a rhythm; fights the boredom that kills long-tail retention.
- **Gives purchases a "why"** — a SKU launched attached to a moment sells; the same SKU as a bare catalog row doesn't. (This is Justin's core thesis.)
- **Honest FOMO** — scarcity that RETURNS and is announced creates urgency without feeling like a trap.

## Real examples (verified Sept 2026)
- **Grow a Garden** — weekly Saturday ~7am PT updates; the whole community organizes around them. Offline growth respects bedtime; the event is the heartbeat.
- **Steal a Brainrot** — scheduled "Admin Abuse" giveaway hours + "Taco Tuesday"; appointment viewing that GIVES. Also ran a Bruno Mars concert (12.8M CCU).
- Pattern that wins: recurring + scheduled + announced with a countdown + generous.

## Merit conditions (add it only if…)
- It's **recurring on a rhythm**, not a one-off dump — the cadence is the value.
- It **ties to systems the game already has** rather than bolting on something random.
- It **gives** (a reward, a spectacle, a fresh challenge) rather than pressuring.
- You can sustain the cadence — a weekly event you skip twice is worse than no event.

## Failure modes
- **Appointment timers that punish absence** (mobile-gacha "log in daily or lose your streak/pet") — reads as coercion; kids and Roblox both punish it.
- **Pay-to-participate** events that gate the fun behind a purchase.
- **One-and-done** events with no cadence — a spike, then a cliff.

## Applied to Layer Mine (Hunter's game)
**Weekly Saturday "Motherlode"** — a temporary super-rich vein spawns at extreme depth for the weekend, with elevated heat. It (a) rewards the shield investment players already made, (b) is a skill/risk spike, not RNG, (c) creates the natural weekly reset that pairs with the weekly leaderboard, and (d) gives the shield-upgrade SKU an obvious "why" that week. One event reinforces heat + shields + leaderboard at once — that's the test of a good event: it makes existing systems matter more, it doesn't add a new one.
