## The one line that matters

**This game has 554 CCU and 5.5M favorites; every "build a rocket and launch it" game on the platform has under 35 CCU.** The difference is not art and it is not marketing. It is that launching is a *novelty verb* — you do it, you read the number, you leave — and mining/selling/upgrading/collecting is a *repeatable verb*. The Space Simulator is a collect-and-upgrade game wearing a space skin. The launch games are launch games.

Anything we build in this family must put a repeatable verb underneath the launch, or it joins the graveyard below.
