## 1. What the test was actually testing

The prompt says it plainly: can the factory take 99 Nights' *machine* and make a different game from it. The honest answer after one session:

- **The design side worked as advertised.** The packet arrived in the factory's own shape (six markers found by `intake_zip.py`), so nothing was re-derived. The brief, the cut line, the theme, the wedge and the lighting numbers went straight to disk. The only things I did to Justin's design were the two checks the skill says run on his typing too (platform blocks: none real; budget: inside it as drawn).
- **The engine side did not.** `docs/GAME_FACTORY.md` promised "a new game adds files under `games/<slug>/` and changes nothing in `src/core/`". That was true for a second *sweep-and-bank* game. It was false for a survive game, and it was false at the first line of the loader: `ContentLoader.validate` demanded a flood cooldown, upgrade roles, an offline cap, and a payout zone. The engine was one-loop-shaped, and nobody could see it until a differently-shaped title arrived. Filed as E-0007.

So the learning is not "the factory failed"; it is that **a factory with one title in it has an engine that is a generalisation of that title**. The second title is the first real test of the word "generic", and it costs one attended session of engine work. The third title is the test of whether the slots are now right.

## 4. Every stall, and what it cost

### 4.1 The engine refused the pack — ~90 min, the whole engine change
Described in §1 and E-0007. Not a stall in the loop-cap sense; it was the work.

### 4.2 The template SpawnLocation — 3 min
The scratch place still had the baseplate template's `SpawnLocation` at the origin, so the first play session spawned the player *inside* the Wick disc instead of 30 studs out. Removed `Baseplate` and `SpawnLocation` from the scratch place in Edit mode. Reversible, unsaved place.

### 4.3 Beat 3 red: standing on the Wick did nothing — 10 min
`ZoneService.contains` is an object-space box test. The feed disc is a cylinder rotated 90° so its thin local X axis points up; a player 3.5 studs above the disc fails the X test. Fixed generically (radial test for vertical cylinders), re-synced, green on the rerun. E-0009. Fat Man never had a disc, so the bug had a whole title to hide in.

### 4.4 The sim parsed the wrong `value` — 2 min
`survive_sim.py` grabbed `fuel.value` (1) instead of `lure.value` (5). Fixed to parse the `lure` block.

### 4.5 `Lighting.Technology` cannot be set from a script — 0 min
"lacking capability RobloxScript". One-click note for Justin in `lighting.md` and STATE.

### 4.6 The night-2 test landed on Day 3 — 0 min, but honest
The step-out logger assumed the run started when the logger did; it had started ~90 s earlier. The player stepped out during Day 3 (watch mode: correctly no chase), and the hunt + catch happened at Night 3 instead. The result is the same test (hunt outside the light → downed → wake at Wick); the day number in the log is 3, not 2.

### 4.7 Hunter pressed Play — and found two bugs the four-beat did not — 15 min
While I was staging screenshots in Edit mode, a human session started on the scratch place (Studio went to Play; captures went black; I left the session alone). The console shows three runs: the teach worked in 18 s, and two rules were wrong — feeding exactly the night's burn died at the last tick, and a wick at zero at nightfall was never checked. Both fixed on disk (`SurviveLoop`: night-start check, 2 s grace on `emptied`). The lesson for the runbook: **the four-beat verifies the machine; a human at the keyboard verifies the rules.** Filed as E-0010. The clock itself was sampled live at 1×; a "too many weeks in the console" anomaly stays open.

### 4.8 The crafting bench art drop — 20 min, no upload
Justin dropped `RB BUSINESS/HELLWEEK/` with a bench as `.glb` + `.blend` + the **Blender script that generated it** + `crafting-table.md`. The script places ~80 primitives in stud units, so it is a better source than the mesh: `tools/bench_from_blender.py` translates it into `games/hell-week/server/bench.luau` (plain Parts, permanent, diffable, no asset upload, no Studio gate) and `world.luau` places it in Ring A ~12 studs from the Wick with the two "0" plates facing spawn. Blender Z-up → Roblox Y-up, cylinders get the local quarter-turn, cones (none in Roblox) become Neon balls for flames and thin rods for spikes, the 3D "0" text becomes a SurfaceGui on each plate. Two generic loader additions: a `cframe` field for imported placements and a `face` field for signs. **Store-only in Pass 1**: no prompt, no deposit — crafting is parked on the cut list; the note itself allows that. Skulls are stylised with dark-inset eyes (mild fear, no gore).

### 4.9 The opening act — 25 min
Justin asked for a title card at startup: what is happening (largest), a four-line ancient voice (bold, blood-drenched), and its meaning. Built as `src/client/OpeningAct.luau` reading `theme.opening` (generic: any pack can carry one), with the HUD holding toasts and controls until it ends, skip on any input after 1.5 s. Verified from the client DataModel: narration 24 px vs voice 16 px, Creepster face, 40 drip elements, Garamond meaning, clean console.
