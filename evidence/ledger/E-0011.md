Hell Week had been reasoning about safety entirely through light falloff, which is invisible at
midday and a guess at night. Watching rather than reading found this in under twenty minutes; ten
prior public sources had all described the fire's radius without once mentioning that it is drawn
on the ground.

Two secondary corrections to the public-source teardown came out of the same session: the widely
repeated 3:00 day / 1:30 night cadence was not observed, and night is navy and fully navigable
rather than black.

Caveat on scope: the subject was a veteran with a 500-diamond class, playing solo, and the session
ended without a death. Every finding here is about moment-to-moment legibility. The meta loop,
the shop, the lobby and the death screen remain unobserved.
