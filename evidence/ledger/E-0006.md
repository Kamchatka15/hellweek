A simulation, so it is a design-time finding and is filed as **observed**, not confirmed. What it does establish without players: the first-minute arithmetic in the config header is correct (28s to first upgrade) and the second-week arithmetic was never done.

Candidate fix (proposed for G2): offline 0.5 bursts/hour, deeper ladders (bag 16 / payout 14 / shine 10 / magnet 8), late growth ~2.0–2.3. Keeps the first minute, pushes "everything maxed" past day 30, offline share ~47%.

Residual: exponential ladders produce a 4–6 session wall around week three in every tuning. Needs a second sink, not a fifth upgrade — a G2 / live-ops decision.

What would confirm this: Gate B analytics showing day-3+ players with nothing to buy, or offline credit dwarfing session earnings in the funnel events.
