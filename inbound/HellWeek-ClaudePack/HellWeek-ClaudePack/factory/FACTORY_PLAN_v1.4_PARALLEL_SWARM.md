## Answer first

**Do not 100% copy a live game and then pull it apart.**

That means taking another creator's place — meshes, audio, scripts, names, map — as the starting file and reskinning it. It is slower (you throw away the engine and spend Pass 2 decontaminating IP), more fragile (you inherit their economy wall and their fading wave), and it is how titles get deleted after they finally have metrics. Audio is fingerprinted. Owned names and characters come down on sight. Extraction tooling risks the account.

**Do use a two-pass, which is the speed idea underneath the bad tactic:**

- **Pass 1 — Trace.** From public play and public video, reconstruct the *loop* on *your* engine: first 60 seconds, verb cadence, how the bag teaches the bank, first purchase moment, appointment timer. Legal Store stand-in art only.
- **Pass 2 — Unique.** Prepared *while* Pass 1 is being built: blended style from 3–5 comps, new names, destination building, one clip/social wedge taken from what players complain the comps lack, economy numbers from *your* sim.
- **Assembly.** Snap Pass 2 files onto the already-green Pass 1 loop. Re-run MCP four-beat.

Copy the machine. Do not steal the place.
